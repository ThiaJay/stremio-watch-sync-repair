# Branding

Stremio Watch Sync & Repair uses an original community-project identity. It does **not** use or reproduce the official Stremio, Trakt or TMDB logos as its product mark.

## Assets

- `assets/icon.svg` — square application/project icon.
- `assets/logo.svg` — horizontal project lockup.
- `assets/release-banner.svg` — 2:1 release/README banner.
- `web/assets/tmdb-primary-short-blue.svg` — TMDB's approved attribution asset, used only in About/Credits.

## Palette

- Deep slate: `#10263E`
- Cyan: `#26D9F3`
- Blue: `#2388F2`
- Teal: `#18CFB9`
- Green: `#45EE84`
- Off-white: `#F7FBFF`

## Usage

Keep the project name legible and retain the sync/repair meaning of the circular-arrow/check symbol. Do not recolour third-party attribution assets. Do not imply affiliation with Stremio, Trakt or TMDB.

The source SVGs are preferred for community posts and documentation because they remain sharp at any size.
